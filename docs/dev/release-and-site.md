# CI, releases and the project page

## CI and releases

CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) runs clippy with `-D warnings` and the tests on Windows and macOS, and the three windowless crates on Linux. A release is a pushed tag `v<version>` that matches the version in `Cargo.toml` (`release.yml`; notes from `.github/release-notes/v<version>.md`). It is always signed, the way BiblioCell does it: `scripts/package-macos.sh --arch universal` builds, signs with the Developer ID certificate, notarizes and staples the app and the DMG; `scripts/package-windows.ps1 -Sign` signs both exes through SSL.com eSigner (`scripts/sign-windows-esigner.ps1`), zips their folder (the portable package) and compiles the setup from the same folder (`scripts/wisp-paper.iss`, Inno Setup 6: for the current user, into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Wisp Paper`, so the app can replace its own files there; the setup is signed, its uninstaller is not), and the workflow installs and removes it once without a question; Linux is an unsigned tar.gz. The ten secrets are listed at the top of `release.yml`; with one missing the run stops before building. A pull request touching `scripts/` or the workflow rehearses the packaging unsigned. Locally `CARGO="cargo +stable" scripts/package-macos.sh` gives an ad hoc signed `dist/Wisp Paper.app`.

## `site/`: the project page

https://xuzhougeng.github.io/WispPaper/ is hand-written files: the front page (`index.html`, `style.css`, `site.js`) and the tutorials (`tutorials/*.html`, `tutorial.css`), no generator and nothing loaded from another host (no web fonts: the stacks are system ones). `site/build.sh [folder]` adds the screenshots from `docs/images` and the icon; `python3 -m http.server --directory dist/site` shows the result. `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys it on a push to main that touches it and after every published release, writing `release.json` (the latest release's files) next to the page: the download buttons read it, and lead to the release page without it. The page is laid out as a paper (numbered figures, citations that can be clicked); its numbers are those of `docs/TUTORIAL.md`, and its colours are `theme.rs`'s. Keep it out of `scripts/`, whose changes rehearse the packaging.

### The tutorials

`site/tutorials/` holds one page per scenario and `index.html`, their list; the front page leads to them from the end of §1 (`.guides`) and its top bar. A tutorial is a record of a real run on the demo topic (the library 「植物单细胞转录组」), told as numbered sections with screenshots. A new one is a copy of `site/tutorials/_template.html`, which shows every element a tutorial may use (`tutorial.css` styles them from `style.css`'s tokens; a page carries no styles or scripts of its own, and `build.sh` leaves files starting with `_` out of the site). Its pictures are `docs/images/tutorials/<slug>-NN-<name>.jpg`, served as `img/t/…`: taken with the `shot:` step of `WISP_PAPER_DEV` (see `debugging.md`) against a `WISP_PAPER_HOME` of its own and scaled with `sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 72 -Z 1372`. The top bar, the footer and the pager are repeated in every page: a change to one is a change to all of them, and a new tutorial also needs its entry in `tutorials/index.html`, in the front page's `.guides`, and in the pager of its neighbours. What a tutorial says about the window has to be checked against the window: labels are quoted in 「」 as displayed.
