# Window notes (root crate `wisp_study`)

Per-feature detail of the GPUI app moved out of `CLAUDE.md`. Read the section for the feature you are about to change; the patterns every feature follows (`Cmd`, `Workspace::change`, background tasks, theme, shortcuts) are in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Conversations and the assistant (`chat.rs`)

A library question's scope is the currently shown list (`lib.shown`), so view, search and filters change what the model reads up front. The context is built once with the first question of a conversation. The panel behind 文献 in the conversation's header (`render_materials` in `chat.rs`, `Chat::materials_open`) floats over the conversation and pushes nothing down; a press beside it or Esc puts it away. Before the first question its search box (`Library::scope_find`, the words in `scope_words`, the papers holding them in `scope_hits`, made anew by `scope_refilter` with the words and with every `refilter`: a frame does not search abstracts) narrows the list and the scope with it (`Library::scope_listed`). A row dragged into the conversation (GPUI's `on_drag` with a `chat::PaperDrag`, dropped anywhere under the header; `Workspace::pinning` while it is held) or its plus keeps the question to that paper: it joins `Library::asked`, the same list 就这 N 篇提问 fills, shown as chips over the question box that let each go again (`Cmd::Scope(ScopeDo)`). `Library::scope_papers` is the one place that says what the scope is (`ask` reads the same fields); a new conversation empties both (`scope_reset`), and once the material is built the panel lists what was handed over and nothing can be picked. `Workspace::save_chat` keeps the conversation in the library after the question and after the answer; 历史 lists them (`Chat::history`) and `open_chat` takes one up again.

What a conversation says and the box under it keep to one column (`chat::reading_column`): at most the reading width of 设置 › 外观 (`theme::manuscript_width`, the manuscript's), centred in a wider page, so a large window leaves its room at the sides instead of making lines longer; the box's card and the answers share their edges (the box's own padding is counted outside the column). The header row stays as wide as the page with its line, and the scroll stays the page's (its bar at the page's edge). The column is a maximum: where `render_chat` is drawn in a narrow place (a paper's conversation in its panel, beside its PDF or floating, 助手写 beside the manuscript, a draft under review) it is that place. It grows from a row (`flex_1` with a `max_w`), never from a percentage, since a wrapping text field under widths that are all percentages is measured at 240px. A table in an answer is as wide as the column.

A library has three conversations, told apart by `Talk`: `Library` (助手, `library_chat`), `Paper` (the selected paper's, `paper_chat`) and `Write` (the one that writes the manuscript, `write_chat`). Every function and command of the chat takes a `Talk`; `chat_of!(lib, talk)` borrows the one meant alone, so the rest of the library can be read meanwhile. What only the library's conversation has (the scope panel, reviews, 插入到稿件) is asked as `talk == Talk::Library`, not as "not the paper's".

The chat is an agent loop (`ask` → `chat_step` → `run_tools` → `execute_tools` → `chat_step` …, at most `MAX_CHAT_STEPS` requests per question; before the last one the results carry `ai::LAST_STEP_NOTE`, telling the model to answer with what it has). `Chat::wire` is the conversation in the protocol's own messages and is append-only: the model's turns go back exactly as received (`ai::TurnReader` keeps thinking blocks and their signatures), and every tool call gets a result even when it did not run: refused by the person (`Chat::pending`, `Cmd::AnswerTools`), or stopped (`stop_chat`, which also keeps the text so far as the model's turn). Tools run on the background executor, each call with its own `Store` connection: a turn that only reads runs its calls side by side, one that writes runs them in order. `wisp_net::stream_chat` sends a request again, up to twice, when the service is busy (408, 409, 429, 5xx) or unreachable. Papers are named to the model by label (`P3`): `ai::resolve_labels` on the way in, `ai::add_labels` on the way out.

A question may carry pictures (`ChatItem::User::images`, pasted into the box or picked): `picture::prepare` makes each one a model takes (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP; scaled down when large), `ai::user_message_with` writes them the way each protocol wants.

A tool that writes a new file outside the library (`PLACED` in `app.rs`: `restyle_docx`, `make_slides`) is not asked about on a card: before it runs the person says where the file goes in a save dialog (`place_outputs`), which is their yes; closing the dialog is a no to that call. The dialog opens beside the Word file given, or in the person's documents for slides, with the deck's title (or the paper's) as the file's name; a file already there is not written over, the new one gets a free name. A scripted run writes without a dialog, slides into the data folder.

A question of the library's conversation may also carry Word files (`Chat::documents`, `ChatItem::User::files`, `Said::User::files`: paths, nothing is copied): a `.docx` dropped on 助手 (`import_paths`; on any other page it is still a manuscript), picked with the plus (`Cmd::AttachImages`, which offers `.docx` for `Talk::Library`) or through the start page's line (`Start::Pick`). It shows as a chip over the box (`document_chip`, `Cmd::RemoveDocument`) and on the question; `send_question` adds `wisp_core::restyle::note` to what the model is sent (the paths, and how to read and restyle them with `read_docx_citations`, `restyle_docx` and the style tools), a file sent without words asks for its citations to be looked at, and 撤回 / 重新回答 carry the files along. `restyle_docx` is not asked about on a card: before it runs, `place_outputs` opens the save dialog beside the file given (`<name>-<style>.docx`), which is the person's yes; closing it answers the call with a refusal, a file already there is never written over (the new one gets a free name, `name (2).docx`), and the model's own `output` is not used. In a scripted run there is no dialog and the file goes beside the source. Asked to put the file into 写作, the assistant calls `import_manuscript` (asked about like any import); once it ran, `execute_tools` calls `manuscript_arrived` (`src/comments.rs`): the manuscripts are read again and 写作 shows the new one at the section `opening_section` picks, as its own 导入 does. The page stays where the person is, and while they are on 写作 in a manuscript that is still listed, the new one is only listed.

The conversation with the library is the page 助手 (`Page::Ask`; it was 向文献库提问 until what it does outgrew the name; the paper's tab is still 提问). Before anything is said it shows what the assistant can be asked to do (`render_start` in `chat.rs`): cards by kind (找文献, 读文献, 整理, 写), two to a row whose lines are either a request sent as it stands (`Cmd::Suggest`) or a slash command left in the box for its words (`Cmd::StartCommand`, its description read off `ai::SLASH`). A card has a width of its own: one that shares the row's width is measured before its lines wrap. `Task::Chat` is labelled 助手 as well, and each task's row in 设置 › AI says which features use its model; a new feature that calls a model is added to that sentence.

The question box of every conversation (`render_composer`) is a scroll area of its own, at most 170px high (`Chat::input_scroll`): the field is given that handle as `TextInput::follow`, and its prepaint scrolls it to keep the caret in sight whenever the caret, the length of the text or the field's width changed since the last time; between such changes the wheel is the person's. The model's name under the box opens `MenuKind::TaskModel(Task::Chat)`, the same menu as 设置 › AI › 任务's row for 助手: the pick is the setting, for all three conversations, and a conversation going on with a model of another protocol is replayed from its text (`replay` in `ask`).

Slash commands are the `ai::SLASH` table (name, description, what follows it, prompt template; an empty template is a command the window carries out, `/new`). `ai::slash_matches` is what the popup over the box offers; while it shows, `TextInput::choosing` hands Up, Down, Tab and Escape to `Workspace::chat_input`. A new command is one entry in the table. The person's own commands are `Settings::commands` (`ai::Command`: name, description, template; 设置 › AI › 命令), read after the built-in ones by `ai::read_input`, which therefore cannot be replaced; `{}` in a template is what follows the name, and a template without it takes what follows at its end. A question keeps what was sent for it (`ChatItem::User::prompt`, `Said::User::prompt`, empty when that is the text), so replaying a conversation does not read a template again; only a question from before that is expanded anew (`ai::expand_slash`, built-in commands only).

An answer is drawn as Markdown: `wisp_core::markdown::parse` (blocks and inline marks, rules ported from quiet.md; what does not close stays as written, since an answer is drawn while it streams) and `src/markdown.rs` (`render`). Citations like `[P3 p.5 "…"]` stay ordinary text; the chips under an answer come from `ai::citations`, the first `SOURCES_SHOWN` of them until `Chat::sources_open` holds the answer's index.

What can be done with a question or an answer as a whole (`render_messages`): under a question, while the pointer is over it (a `group_hover` row) and no answer is on its way, 复制 and 撤回 (`Cmd::Retract`, `Workspace::retract`); under an answer, 复制, 重新回答 on the last one (`Cmd::Regenerate`) and 从这里分支 (`Cmd::Fork`, `fork_chat`; not in a review's conversation, whose record belongs to one conversation). All three end in `Chat::cut`, which takes what follows a place out of `messages` and empties `wire` with `protocol = None`, so the next request replays the conversation from its text, as after a change of protocol: `wire` stays append-only between cuts. 撤回 puts the question's words and pictures in the box (a draft follows them) and keeps what it took as `Chat::retracted` until the next question is sent, so 恢复 under the conversation (`Cmd::Unretract`) puts it back with the box as it was; a second 撤回 further up takes the first along. A conversation left empty forgets its context, so the scope can be chosen again. 重新回答 goes through `send_question` with the question's own words and pictures (`again`), leaving the box alone, under the material the conversation already has; if nothing could be sent the conversation is put back as it was. 分支 saves the conversation as it stands, then the part up to the answer is kept under a new id (its pictures again, `allow_changes` not carried over) and is the one on show. None of them undoes what a tool did to the library.

## The home page's assistant and drafts (`draft.rs`)

The home page lists the libraries only, in a fixed order: `libraries::shelf` puts the ids of `Settings::library_order` first and the rest in the order they were made; opening one changes only its `opened_at` (on the card, and the order of 最近对话). A card dragged onto another (`home::LibraryDrag`, `drag_over` tints the target) is `Cmd::MoveLibrary`, which writes the whole shelf back through `libraries::reorder`; the assistant is the round button at its bottom right (drawn as the floating conversation's, `wisp_core::floating::BUTTON`; tooltip 助手), which opens the assistant's page (`Do::Begin`, `Home::open`; also the palette's 助手：新对话, the shortcut `ask` on the home side, and a file dropped on home). That page is a state of the home side (`lib` stays `None`; `render_start_page`): 「← 首页」 at the top left and, in the reading column, the box, the three starts and 最近对话 (`recent_rows`). Esc and 「← 首页」 go back (`Cmd::GoHome` clears `open` and `listing`); what is in the box stays while the app runs; `keep_session` keeps nothing for it, so a restart comes back to home. Its box is `Workspace::home` (`draft::Home`: the input, the files for the first question, `waiting`). Every command is `Cmd::Draft(Do)`. Nothing is made before `draft_send`: it checks the model, makes the draft (`libraries::create_draft`), opens it (`open_library`, which for a draft sets `Page::Ask` and takes its last conversation up again through `open_chat`), puts the words into `library_chat`'s box and asks (`ask_library`). The fourth start, 从 Word 建文献库 (`COLLECT`), picks a Word file and sets `Home::collect`: `draft_send` then names the conversation 「<file> 的参考文献」 and puts the Word files into `library_chat.documents` instead of taking them in as manuscripts, so the question carries `restyle::note` with their paths and the assistant runs `import_docx_references` (the person agrees on the consent card); 建成文献库 is still the person's. Files go first: a Word file through `import_manuscript_files` (in a draft `manuscript_taken` cuts a file without heading styles at the likely headings without the dialog, and `manuscript_import` does not enter 写作), a PDF through `draft_take_pdf` → `add_pdf`; each ends in `draft_file_done`, and the question is sent when `waiting` reaches 0. A file dropped on the home side joins the box and opens the page (`home_attach`, from `import_paths` when no library is open), with words if the box was empty; nothing is sent by itself. `render_home_assistant` and `recent_rows` draw the box, the start lines (`STARTS`) and 最近对话 (`MenuKind::Draft`: 重命名 `Modal::Rename`, 建成文献库, 删除 through `AskRemoveLibrary`, worded for a conversation).

A draft is drawn by `render_draft` instead of any page: `sidebar_width` is 0, `render_corner` has only 「← 首页」, `asking` is false (a paper's conversation stays in its panel), and `run` puts `lib.page` back to `Page::Ask` after any command; the shortcuts that reach the list, the sidebar or 写作 do nothing (`Workspace::shortcut`), the palette offers only 回到首页 / 建成文献库 and lists drafts as 打开对话. `render_chat` for a draft: the draft's name as the title, what it kept as the scope line, 「文献 N」 (the panel lists `lib.papers`, nothing to pin), 历史 (`Do::History`, `Home::listing`: the conversations in its place, `render_draft_history`), 新对话 (`Do::New`: the assistant's page) and 建成文献库; no 保存 / 导出, 从这里分支, nor the start cards (`render_draft_start` instead). Once an answer is in and the draft holds papers, a card under it (`render_promote_offer`) says the home page has none of it until 建成文献库 and offers that dialog (`Do::Promote`) and 稍后 (`Do::Later`: `Home::later` keeps the draft and its paper count, and the card is back when more come in); `ai::draft_instructions` and `library_status`'s `draft` / `note` keep the model from saying the library is made.

The assistant's own card (`offer_action`, any conversation): `execute_tools` keeps the last offer of a step as `Chat::offer` with the number of messages then, and `render_offer` draws it under the answer while no question came after it and nothing is running, in place of the card above. Its buttons are those `Workspace::offer_fits` allows here (建成文献库 only in a draft, the pages and 查找开放全文 only in a library), the first a primary button; a card left with none is not drawn. A press is `Cmd::Offer(talk, Some(n))`: `reply` is `Cmd::Suggest` (the words go as the person's next question), the others `Do::Promote`, `Cmd::FindPdfs(None)`, `Cmd::SetPage`; the × is `Cmd::Offer(talk, None)`, which in a draft is 稍后 as well. The offer is not kept with the conversation. So 「← 首页」 from a draft is one step to home, and changing conversations never passes through it. The consent card says 「收进或改动这次对话里的文献」 there. Once a manuscript with text is there the page is the conversation, `render_manuscript_column` (agent form, without the mode switch) and, from `ROOM_FOR_COMMENTS`, a column of `render_comments`; under `STACK_UNDER` the manuscript stands over the conversation. The widths are fixed shares (no dragged edge). The manuscript's bar says 「稿件 N」 in words even in a narrow column (switching and 移除这份稿件 are there; 新建稿件 is not offered, since an empty manuscript would not stand beside the conversation) and ends in a × (`Do::Manuscript(false)`): it sets `Writing::away` and the conversation has the page, with 稿件 in its header (`Do::Manuscript(true)`) to bring it back; `write_show` clears `away`, so a manuscript chosen, saved from an answer, handed in or commented on comes back by itself. Without room for the comments' column a marker clicked (`comment_point` sets `Writing::side` to comments, `side_open` and `focused`) opens the same list as a drawer over the manuscript's right edge (`render_draft_comments` with `drawer`), closed by its × (`Cmd::WriteSide`). A tool call to `comment_manuscript` ends in `comments_arrived`. 建成文献库 is `library_dialog` for the draft (`Modal::Library::proposing`; the sparkle is `Do::Propose`, `Task::Chat`'s model through `wisp_net::complete`, dropped when the library or the dialog changed meanwhile); submit calls `libraries::promote` and replaces `lib.info`, so the sidebar comes back with the page still 助手 and the conversation's context forgotten to be built as a library's.

Leaving a conversation does not stop it (`Workspace::close_library`, used by `Cmd::GoHome` and `open_library`): a library whose conversation is answering, or waits for a yes to a tool (`Library::answering`), is kept in `Workspace::parked` instead of being dropped. The chat loop's callbacks (`chat_step`, `place_outputs`, `execute_tools`) go through `in_library`, which puts the parked library in `lib`'s place for the call and back after, so what an answer brings is handled the same way on screen and off; when it is no longer answering it is dropped with a toast (`parked_done`). Opening it takes it up as it stands (reloaded, the info from the home page's list); opening the library on show while it answers does nothing. 最近对话 and the library cards mark one at work with 回答中, or 等你同意 (`off_screen_mark`). Removing a library or changing the data folder drops it. Inside a library, 新对话 (and taking up another conversation from 历史) does the same one level down for the conversation with the library: one answering is moved to `Library::aside` (`chat_aside`; `exchange` keeps the box, whose subscription is the place's, where it is) and goes on there. Each `Chat` has a `key` for the window's run, which the loop's callbacks carry: `in_chat` finds the conversation in its place or exchanges the one set aside into it for the call, and lets it go with a toast once it is no longer answering (kept by `save_chat` as it went). 历史 marks it 回答中 / 等你同意 (`at_work_mark`) and a click takes it up as it stands (`take_aside`); opening the conversation on show while it answers only closes 历史; removing it drops it. A library with a conversation aside counts as answering, so leaving it parks it too. The paper's conversation and the one that writes are still stopped by 新对话: what they are about (the paper selected, the manuscript on show) can change under them.

## 写作 (`write.rs`, `manuscripts.rs`, `write_find.rs`, `write_assist.rs`, `write_cite.rs`)

写作 (`src/write.rs` renders the editor and the candidates; `src/manuscripts.rs` the manuscripts; state in `Writing`): a library holds any number of manuscripts (`wisp_core::manuscript`: tables `manuscripts`, `manuscript_sections`, `section_snapshots`, with the `Store` methods in that file). A manuscript has a title, a language (`Writing::language`, a code of `manuscript::LANGUAGES`, default `en`) and ordered sections; a section has the person's name and a `kind`, a key of `structure::SECTIONS`, which is the part the candidates come from (`Writing::kind`; `other` for a review's own sections). A new one starts with IMRaD named in its language (`manuscript::imrad`). 写作 opened in a library without a manuscript makes an empty one to write in (`write_ensure`); when a real one then comes (新建稿件, a file, a review), `write_retire_blank` puts the untouched empty one away (`Store::retire_blank_manuscripts`). A section's text is saved as it is typed (`Workspace::write_typed`); a snapshot is kept `PAUSE_SECS` after the last key and on leaving the section (`write_snapshot`), only when `manuscript::worth_keeping`, at most `KEEP_SNAPSHOTS`; restoring one (`Store::restore_snapshot`) first keeps the current text. A removed manuscript is only marked (`removed_at`); a removed section goes with its snapshots, after a confirmation when it has text. Export (`manuscript::markdown`, `plain_text` for 复制全文, `docx`, a hand-written zip with stored entries) leaves out empty sections. A Word file cites for a reader, who has no keys: `Store::manuscript_word` writes each citation group whose keys all name papers in `Settings::cite_style` (a key of `cite::Style` read by `csl::style`: the two simple forms, a built-in CSL style of `csl::common`, or one the person added with 添加引用样式文件, `Cmd::AddCiteStyle`, or Zotero has; chosen at the foot of 导出's menu, `Cmd::CiteStyle`) through `cite::Formatter`, made once from every section so numbers and letters run through the manuscript: `(Zhang et al., 2024a; Li & Wang, 2019)` by author and year (full-width brackets and `；` in a Chinese manuscript, `张等` for a Chinese name), or `[1, 3–5]` in the order first cited; a group with a key nothing has stays as written, a page or a quotation in a citation is left out, each citation is a field that jumps to its paper's entry, and the list is a last section under References / 参考文献 (comment quotes are written the same way so they are found again). The manuscript keeps its keys, and the Markdown export stays Pandoc's. 预览 (`Writing::reading`, `Cmd::WriteReading`) is the eye in the manuscript's bar left of the language, lit while on: the whole manuscript with its Markdown drawn (`render_manuscript_reading`); a section's name goes back to writing it. It is a way of showing the manuscript, so it sits with the manuscript and not among the outline's rows. The `drafts` table of earlier builds is emptied into one manuscript (`早先的草稿`) by `manuscript::migrate_drafts` on every open, so what an older build writes there comes in as another one; the schema version stays, since an older build reads the rest as before. Candidates are asked for, not looked up while typing (`src/write_find.rs`, `CandidateSearch`): the shortcut `write-find` (⌘↩ / Ctrl+Enter) or the panel's button searches for the selection, else the paragraph at the caret (`retrieval::paragraph_at`); the find box searches on Enter, its words as typed when they are plain Latin. Only the list of how each paper opens the part comes by itself (`write_openers`, when a part is opened), and texts read or sorted anew refresh only that list (`write_texts_changed`). What is written, in any language, is made by the translation model into three to five groups of search words in the sentences' language (`retrieval::queries_system` / `parse_groups`: content, rhetorical move, other wordings; cached per language and text in `CandidateSearch::made`), shown under the find box; without a model the words go as written and the panel says so. The part and the sentences' language are read through `Writing::kind` and `Library::sentences_language`. The `other` part is offered when some text has sentences there; the candidates can be narrowed to a tag or the starred papers, and a card unfolds into its neighbours. The model's part in the groundwork is a run like the screening one (`write_run_start` / `write_run_next`, `Task::Structure`): sorting asks about a text's outline (`structure::outline_prompt` / `parse_outline`) only for files whose rules left `OPEN_ENOUGH` paragraphs guessed, and `structure::merge` keeps the rules' labels where they were sure; reading sends each page of a file without text as a picture (`pdf::page_pictures`, `ai::read_page_request`), at most `READ_PAGES` per run, and keeps the text with `Store::save_read_pages`, which records who read it in `structures.text_by` and how many of how many pages in `pages_read` / `page_count`: a file read in part (`StructureInfo::read_in_part`) says so in the run's result and the detail panel, and the next run goes on from the first page not read. The actions on the paragraph being written are `src/write_assist.rs` (`Writing::assist`), on the selection or the caret's paragraph (`ai::paragraph_at`, `write_assist::target`), and of two kinds that the bar (`render_assist_bar`: groups a line apart, each button with a tooltip saying what comes back) and the card keep apart. Looking in the library changes nothing: 找句子 (`Cmd::WriteFind`, the candidates) and 配引用. A new version of the words is for the person to take or leave: 润色, 译成稿件语言, 仿写 (`ai::Assist::Rewrite`: rewritten after the library's sentences closest to the paragraph; when the candidates on show are not those, they are searched for first, `Assisting::awaiting`, and `write_rank` goes on with `assist_begin`; a paper drawn on is cited as `[@Key]`, `ai::Example::key`) and the person's own commands; `ai::assist_prompt` / `assist_request`, `Task::Chat`'s model, streamed with `wisp_net::stream_chat`. The result streams into a card over either page of the side panel, beside the original with the changed words marked (`ai::word_diff`; none for a translation or an own command); only the person replaces the paragraph (found again by its text, one step for `TextInput`'s Undo), inserts it below, copies or drops it. 配引用 (`src/write_cite.rs`, `wisp_core::citefind`, `Card::cite`) never shows a version of the paragraph: off the main thread, the translation model says what each sentence does and turns it into search words in the language of the library's papers (`citefind::plan_prompt`, so a Chinese paragraph finds English papers), the tool `find_citations` ranks the papers for each sentence, and the chat model says which of them bear it out (`judge_prompt` / `parse_judgement`); the card lists, under each sentence (with what it does as a small label), the papers with why and the sentence of their own that says so, the model's choices ticked and said to be 原始研究 or 综述 (without a model: all unticked). A paper that only restates the sentence is a line under it (`render_cite_hint`) naming the works it cites for it, and those the library holds are offered unticked (`Offer::via`); when the model chose nothing one line says so and 看相近的 opens what was found by words (`Found::leads`, `turn_rest`); the card ends with what was searched, and 插入 writes `[@Key]` at the ends of the sentences with `citefind::placed` and `replace_apart` (`Cmd::WriteCited`). One request at a time; a new part (`Assisting::leave`), library or request drops what is in flight. The manuscript's language is `Workspace::manuscript_language`, English until the manuscript carries one. Under the manuscript's bar the column is a sheet (`render_manuscript_column`): as wide as the reading width (`theme::manuscript_width`) with `SHEET_MARGIN` at each side, centred, and the actions, the source strip and the box of 改稿 under it keep its edges, so a wide window leaves its room beside the sheet and not inside it. The section being written and the manuscript read through have no frame of their own: the column's lines bound them, and one line under them is where the text scrolls away. A page narrower than `STACK_UNDER` (a small window, or a paper open at the right) has no room for the manuscript and the panel side by side: the manuscript has the page and the panel is a drawer under it (`render_candidates` with `stacked`), its row of tabs while folded and `DRAWER_SHARE` of the height while open (`Writing::side_open`: a tab opens its page, the tab on show or the chevron folds it, and whatever turns the panel to a page, 找句子, an action's card, a comment pointed at, opens it). Each of the two scrolls by itself; the page never scrolls both.

Searching the manuscript (`src/write_seek.rs`, state `Writing::seek`, a `Seek`; commands `Cmd::Seek(Do)`): on the writing page the shortcut `search` opens a bar over the manuscript column (`render_seek`, both modes) instead of focusing the papers' search box (`Workspace::shortcut`). `manuscript::occurrences` finds the words in each section without case (`seek_hits`, the section on show as the editor has it); the ones of the section on show are tinted after the comments' and the revisions' marks (`seek_marks`, `Theme::found` / `found_current`), so a click on words is still a comment's. Typing selects the first place from the selection on, in that section only; Enter and the arrows (`seek_step`) go round the whole manuscript, `seek_go` showing another section through `write_show` and leaving 预览. The scroll waits for the editor to draw the section (`seek_reveal`): `TextInput::set_text` drops the old layout, so `top_of` is `None` until then. Esc reaches `Workspace::dismiss`, which puts the bar away before a paper beside it is closed. The shortcut `reading` on the writing page, with no paper beside it, is 预览.

## 助手写 (`write_agent.rs`)

## 改稿 (`revise.rs`)

改稿 (`src/revise.rs`, state `Writing::revise`, a `Revising`; every command is `Cmd::Revise(Do)`): the box under the manuscript on the writing page. ## Citations and 出处 (`cite.rs`, `write_source.rs`)

Citations in 写作 (`wisp_core::cite`, window side `src/cite.rs`, state `Library::citing`): written in Pandoc's form `[@Zhang2024]` (`[@A; @B]`, with locators), so a draft and its exported Markdown stay plain text and Pandoc formats them with the BibTeX of the cited papers (`cite::bibtex`, same keys, through `export::bibtex_keyed`). A key is first author + year (`DeNovo2020` from the title without authors, `-nd` without a year), a letter for the second paper with the same key; `cite::Index` gives them in the order papers were added, so a new paper never takes an earlier one's key, and a key once cited is kept in `cite_keys` (`Store::keep_cite_key`) so it stays with its paper when the record changes or another is deleted. `cite::find` resolves every citation of a text (`Found`: paper, trashed, removed, unknown, or ambiguous for the old `(Zhang et al., 2021)` form, which is still read and `cite::upgrades` rewrites into keys); `cite::references` is the list in author-year order, `reference_list` / `reference_markdown` its text. Functions take text, one section or several, never the drafts table. 引用 (`Cmd::WriteCite`, a paper id) inserts the key; the reference list is the third page of the panel beside the manuscript (`render_references`): of the part or the whole manuscript, it leads to each paper, says in the whole manuscript's list which sections cite it, points out what does not resolve (a dot on the page's tab), and offers any paper of the library by typing part of it (`Index::matching`). The outline's row 参考文献 (`Cmd::WriteReferences`) shows the whole manuscript's list, and a manuscript read through ends in it (`render_references_read`), which is where that row scrolls to then. In 助手写 there is no panel beside the manuscript: the row opens the list as a column at the right of the page (`render_agent_references`, `agent_references_width`, open while `Writing::side` is `References`) and closes it again, as does its ×; the edge between conversation and manuscript leaves its width out. Where the page has no room for a third column (`agent_references_width` is 0 unless the conversation and the manuscript keep `CHAT_MIN` and `MANUSCRIPT_MIN` beside it) the list stands in the manuscript's place until it is closed. A page narrower than `write_agent::STACK_UNDER` puts the manuscript (`STACKED_SHARE` of the height) over the conversation, each scrolling by itself. What the manuscript cites is worked out once a frame, before the outline and the page are drawn (`cite_refresh` in `render_library`, kept in `Citing::listed`); the tab, the row and the page read that and build no index of their own.

出处 (`src/write_source.rs`, state `Citing::sources`, a `Sourcing`; every command is `Cmd::Source(Do)`): `cite::Listed::standing` is every citation of the manuscript with the passage kept for it (`standing`, a `Standing` with its `Rests`), worked out by `cite_refresh` with the rest. On the reference page beside the manuscript each entry has under it the passages its citations on show rest on (`render_passages`: the paper's whole sentence, part and page, a sparkle on one a model found, in the warning colour a citation whose paper does not bear it out), and a click is `Cmd::GotoSource` with the page and the sentence, which the PDF highlights. With the caret in a citation a line under the manuscript (`render_source_strip`, over the box of 改稿) names the paper and shows that sentence's passage, or the sparkle that looks for it; on the reference page its entry is brought into view once (`source_follow`, the list's `ScrollHandle`) and the passage is lit. A passage is kept when the person cites from a sentence: 引用 on a candidate is `Cmd::WriteCiteFrom` (`cite_paper` with the candidate; a sentence that cites the paper already is not made to cite it twice, the passage is kept for the citation there), 配引用's 插入 keeps each ticked paper's own sentence, and an answer put into a manuscript (`sources_quoted`, from `insert_into_section` and `draft_to_manuscript`) keeps the sentences it quoted where `ai::locate_quote` finds them. The sparkle over the list (with how many citations have none) is a run off the main thread (`sources_find` → `work`): `Task::Translate`'s model makes search words of the citing sentences (`citefind::words_prompt`), the tool `find_passages` looks through each cited paper once, and `Task::Chat`'s model chooses, `AT_ONCE` citations a request; what it finds is kept as it comes (`Store::keep_passages`), at most `passage::MAX_ASKED` a run, and a toast sums it up. Without a model the nearest sentence is kept when `fair`; a paper with text and not a sentence sharing a word is kept as not bearing the citation out. The × on a passage's line lets go of it. An entry's own row and each passage's line are separate elements: a click handler on a parent would hear the child's click too.

Pointing at a citation in the editor (`write_source.rs`): the editor is given the citation groups of the section (`TextInput::set_links`, from `Listed::cited_here`, by `citation_links`) and keeps where each was drawn (`link_boxes`, window coordinates, made in prepaint). The pointer over one tints it (`Theme::link_hover`) and emits `InputEvent::LinkHovered`; `render_citation_hint` then draws a card under the link's last line (`hovered_link` reaches down to it, so the card never covers the citation): each paper named, with key, authors and year, title, journal, and the sentence it rests on, the quotation written in the citation (`cite::said`: `[@Key p.2 "…"]`, page and quote) or else the passage kept for it, and what ⌘ / Ctrl with a click does. 引用卡片 at the left of the bar under the manuscript (`Cmd::CitationCards`, saved as `Settings::citation_cards_hidden`) turns the card off; the link is still tinted and followed. That click (`InputEvent::LinkClicked`, caught in `on_down` before the sentence selection the same keys make elsewhere) is `citation_follow`: `Cmd::GotoSource` with the page and sentence, so the PDF opens in the detail panel at the right with the sentence lit, or the paper alone when no page is known; a key nothing has is said in a toast. The hover is let go of when the pointer leaves the field, the wheel turns, the text changes, a click follows the link, or the link's box moves (the page around it changed).

## The manuscript editor and text fields (`input.rs`)

The manuscript editor draws Markdown as it says (`TextInput::markdown`, like quiet-md's editor but keeping the text as written): `wisp_core::markdown::lines` reads each line with its pieces and where their syntax is (`Piece`, `Part`; `inline` is built on the same reader), `shape_markdown` in `input.rs` shapes one row per line (headings larger, emphasis, code, links and `[@Key]` citations in their style, a dot for `-`, quotes behind a bar, a ground for fenced code, a line for `---`) and hides the syntax except on the lines the selection or caret is on (held while the button is down). `DisplayMap` is how drawn offsets stand to the text's bytes; every hit test, caret, selection, IME rectangle and comment tint goes through it. Enter in a list or quote goes on with it (`markdown::continued`), on an empty item ends it. A section's text is therefore Markdown everywhere: `manuscript::section_markdown` (headings written in a section go under the section's) is the export and what 通读 and the versions draw, `markdown::plain` is the clipboard's and a Word file's text, a new Word file gets the bold, italic and headings (`word_paragraph`), and a Word file's paragraph that would read as Markdown comes in escaped (`escape_markdown`).

What a click selects in a text field is `wisp_core::selection`: two clicks a word (ICU4X's `icu_segmenter` with its dictionaries, so Chinese and Japanese come apart into words; single Han characters next to each other, an unknown term, are taken together up to a word in `JOINING`; a `[@…]` citation is one word), ⌘ / Ctrl with a click the sentence (`citefind::whole_sentences`: cut as 配引用 cuts, but a semicolon goes on), three the paragraph (the line without its quote, marker or hashes). `TextInput::grain` keeps the unit, and a drag after the press grows the selection by it (`selection::extend`). A masked field selects all.

`input.rs` is the app's own text input (single / multi-line, IME via `EntityInputHandler`; a field that `accepts_images` hands pasted pictures to its owner; Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Z undo and redo, letters typed in a row being one step; `replace_range` is an owner's change as one step); `pdf.rs` renders pages with `hayro` on a worker thread into a virtualised list, keeping a bounded number of pages.

## 综述

综述 (`wisp_core::review`, window side in `src/chat.rs`): `/review` gives the conversation with the library a record (`review_projects`, one JSON body with a `revision`; `review_conversations` ties it to its conversation, and it goes with it: `Store::remove_conversation`, and the schema batch clears one left behind). `Review::step` is where it stands (1 the requirements, 2 the framework, 3 the outline, 4 writing); `ReviewAction` is every change, applied by `Store::apply_review` against the revision the caller read. The outline comes in two stages, after gzd-review-writing's F0 → F1: `Review::framework` (初步框架) is what the reviews already published make of the topic and the chapters proposed from them, a starting point nothing has borne out; the outline is then built against it from the library's own papers, chapter by chapter (kept, merged, split, dropped, new, and which papers made it so). The person may go on without a framework (`ReviewAction::SkipFramework`, only while none is recorded), and a record from before it, which has an outline and no framework, counts as one that did (`Review::framework_settled`). A framework worded anew asks for its confirmation and the outline's again. Only the person advances it, and by one click: taking an answer (or what is selected in one: `Cmd::Review(ReviewDo, text)`) for the requirements, the framework or the outline records and confirms it (`review_do` applies the `Set…` and the `Confirm…` action); a text written or changed in the record is confirmed by itself, at its heading there or in the card. No section is kept before the outline is confirmed. The line over the conversation only says where the review stands among its four steps (`REVIEW_STEPS`); the person's move is one card at the end of the conversation (`render_review_next`), under the answer it is about: take that answer (`review_offer`: the whole of the last answer, when it came after the review's last step, `Chat::review_mark`, and the record does not hold it, `review_holds`), confirm a text that waits, or send the request that asks for the step's text (`Cmd::Suggest`). The answer the card offers has no button of its own; earlier answers keep one, under the same name (`review_take`). Since the click keeps the model's last answer as it stands, `review_note` tells the model to give the whole text every time and names the button, and the first step of the `/review` template asks for a list that can be taken as it is. A section kept (`ReviewDo::Section`, `section_of`) brings its evidence: `review::cited_evidence` makes a row of every citation (the sentence the citation closes, the paper behind the label, page, quotation), added once and unjudged; `Library::load_review` reads the record into `Chat::review` and looks the quotations up with `ai::check_quote` (`Chat::review_checks`) whenever the conversation, the record or the papers change, so drawing asks the store nothing. Whether a source bears a claim out is the person's (`EvidenceJudgment`, `Cmd::JudgeReviewEvidence`); a judgment keeps `Store::evidence_fingerprint` (title, abstract, the text of the page named) and stands while that and the claim's sentence do (`Review::linked`, `source_current`). There are no triggers and no version bump for any of this: `store::UNRELEASED_VERSION` is what builds between 0.5.0 and the next release wrote, and the schema batch drops what they left. The record is shown in place of the conversation (`Chat::review_open`, `render_review_record`), each text of it changed in `Modal::ReviewText`, a row in `Modal::ReviewEvidence`. A question of such a conversation carries `chat::review_note` (the step and what it asks of the model, the requirements, the framework until the outline is confirmed, the outline, the sections by name), and not the sections' text, with one exception: the question 核对全文 sends (`chat::VERIFY_SECTIONS`, told by `verifies_sections` in either language) is about that text and carries it (`review_sections`), since what is saved is the record's and need not be what the conversation last said; without it the model looked for a manuscript on the writing page and checked its last answer instead. `review_to_manuscript` makes a new manuscript of the sections; `cite::convert_ai_citations` turns `[P3 p.5 "…"]` into `[@Key, p. 5]`, leaving the quotation in the record.

## 筛选 (`screen.rs`, `screen_single.rs`, `screen_confirm.rs`)

筛选 has three faces (`Screen::mode`, a `ScreenMode`; `Cmd::ScreenMode`): 批量 is the queue as a list with the assistant's run over it (`src/screen.rs`); 逐篇 is one paper at a time (`src/screen_single.rs`: a rail of the round's papers with what became of each, the paper with the assistant's suggestion and its abstract, the four decisions under it; a decision brings the next paper up, past the last one the round is summed up; `Library::round_paper` is the paper on show, which is not `Library::selected`: the detail panel opens beside it only when asked, Enter or `Cmd::ScreenBeside`, and then follows the round); 确认 is the suggestions for the waiting papers looked over and written together (`src/screen_confirm.rs`: a row per paper with the status it will get, ticked unless left out; what is on show is written by `Cmd::ConfirmApply` and can be taken back, `Screen::confirmed`). ## 自定义: connectors and skills (`custom.rs`)

自定义 (`src/custom.rs`, 设置 › 自定义; every command is `Cmd::Custom(Do)`): what the person adds to the assistant. 连接器 are MCP servers (`Settings::connectors`, a `connector::Connector`: a name, a target and a token): a target that begins `http(s)://` is asked over Streamable HTTP with the token as a bearer (after `initialize`, in the protocol revision the server answered with), anything else is a command started through the person's login shell (`cmd /C` on Windows) that speaks on its standard streams. An address whose post of `initialize` is answered 400, 404 or 405 is a server of the transport before Streamable HTTP (2024-11-05, the `…/sse` addresses many services still give, as the 2025-03-26 revision has clients fall back): `mcp::Sse` opens the event stream there with a GET, posts every message to the address its `endpoint` event names (`connector::endpoint`: on the same origin only, since the token goes along) and reads the replies, and the pings it answers by post, off the stream (`connector::Events`, a reader thread feeding a channel as for a command); both HTTP links send the token and refresh the sign-in through `mcp::Auth`. `wisp_core::connector` has the messages and how replies are read, `wisp_net::mcp` the three transports and the open connections (`tools` connects anew and lists, `call` connects once more when a connection broke, `keep_only` closes the rest, `disconnect` one; both say `mcp::Failure::SignIn` apart from other failures). A connector is connected to at start, by its switch and by 连接, never while its fields are typed (a command half written is not run); where each stands is `Workspace::connections` (`custom::Connection`). A remote connector without a token of its own is signed in to in the browser (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, as MCP's authorization spec has it): `wisp_core::oauth` is the side without sockets (reading `WWW-Authenticate`, where protected resource and authorization server metadata are looked for, the registration body, the authorize address, token forms and how answers are read, the loopback's callback, the file), `wisp_net::oauth` asks it: `begin` probes the server, finds its metadata (or, for a server from before RFC 9728, its origin's), registers the app as a public client (reusing the client of last time while its loopback port is free) and opens the loopback on `localhost`; the window sends the browser to `SignIn::address` (a scripted run prints it as `sign-in: …` instead), and `SignIn::finish` waits for the callback (`WAIT`, or until `custom::Stop`), exchanges the code and answers the browser with a page that says how it went. The sign-ins are kept by address in `oauth.json` beside `settings.json` (plain text like the model keys, mode 600 where there is such a thing), read and written under one lock; `bearer` is the token a request carries, refreshed first when within `oauth::MARGIN_SECS` of running out, and a 401 is refreshed once (`renew`) before it becomes `Failure::SignIn`; a refresh the server refuses ends the sign-in and keeps the client, one that fails otherwise keeps both. 连接 asked for by the person goes on to the sign-in by itself, a connection at start only says one is wanted (需要在浏览器中登录, with 在浏览器中登录); 退出登录 forgets the tokens and gives them back to the server's revocation endpoint, and removing a connector forgets its sign-in unless another uses the address. A server whose authorization server takes no registrations cannot be signed in to this way: its token goes into 令牌. `cargo run -p wisp_core --example mock_connector -- 8790 [seconds]` is a server that wants a sign-in (`http://localhost:8790/mcp`), with a consent page and tokens good for the given seconds. 技能 are folders with a `SKILL.md` under `skills/` beside `settings.json` (`wisp_core::skill`: front matter `name` and `description`, then the instructions; `Settings::skills_off` are those switched off), read at start and when the page is opened (`Workspace::skills`); a skill is words only, nothing in its folder is run. Both reach the assistant in `chat_step` as tools after the library's (`Workspace::outside_tools`, `ai::chat_request_with`): a connector's tools as `<slug>__<tool>`, and `use_skill`, whose description lists the skills on. `run_tools` looks a call up with `outside_call` (`PreparedCall::outside`); a connector's tool that does not declare `readOnlyHint` is asked about before it runs, on the same card as the tools that change the library, with its arguments as the details. `wisp` and `wisp mcp` know nothing of either.

## Settings (`settings.rs`)

`settings.rs` is the settings view (通用, 外观, 快捷键, AI, 自定义, 网络, 关于). The AI page has three tabs (`AiTab`, `Cmd::AiTab`): 模型 is the providers in a list at the left and the one picked at the right with its models (`ProviderFields`, made anew for the provider on show; a model added keeps them, so the field keeps the keyboard), 任务 which model each task uses with its instructions (the translation prompt), 命令 the person's own commands. A provider's mark is `assets/icons/provider-<preset>.svg` (from lobe-icons, see THIRD_PARTY.md) on a tile of `theme::provider_tile`. The 任务 cards are given their width in pixels (`column_width`): under shares, the column leaves a gap under the card with the prompt. It replaces the page while `Workspace::settings_view` is `Some`; the open library stays underneath. There is no save button: each change writes `settings.json` at once.

Every page stands in one frame, `AI_COLUMN_W` wide and centred; a narrower page (`COLUMN_W`) keeps to the frame's left, so the left edge is the same on every page. 返回 is in the window's top-left corner, as the library's way back is. 外观 has two fonts, 西文字体 (`Settings::font_family`) and 中文字体 (`cjk_font_family`). `theme::ui_font()` is one `Font`: the Western family, with the Chinese one as its only fallback, so a Chinese character is drawn in the Chinese font only where the Western font has no glyph for it; set it with `.font(…)` on a root element. A Western font with Chinese glyphs of its own (苹方, 微软雅黑) therefore still draws Chinese; the 中文字体 row says so (`draws_chinese` shapes 文 and looks at the run's font). Either picker (the button, the search field, the names in the list) is set in `theme::UI_FONT` whatever font is chosen, with a sample in the font itself beside each name (Aa Bb 123 for the Western one, 文献阅读 for the Chinese one): a font that draws letters as pictures would otherwise hide its own name and what is typed. One picker is open at a time, under its own row. Debug steps: `fontpicker` / `fontpicker:cjk`, `fontfamily:<name>`, `cjkfont:<name>`.

A wrapping `TextInput` under ancestors whose widths are all percentages (the settings column) is measured at 240px; give its wrapper a pixel width there, as `ai_page` does for the translation instructions.

## Background and the release check (`background.rs`)

Background (`src/background.rs`): while `Settings::keep_running` is on (设置 › 通用 › 后台, the default), the window's close button does not quit: macOS hides the app (the Dock icon brings it back through `on_reopen`), Windows hides the window behind a tray icon (`tray-icon`; 打开 / 退出) and a second launch for the same settings file brings that window up (`hand_over`, a marker file in the temp folder) instead of starting again. The window and its runs stay as they were. macOS has an app menu for it (⌘Q, ⌘H; `background::set_menus`, made again when the language changes); Linux closes as before.

The release check (`check_update`) reads the redirect of GitHub's `releases/latest` (the JSON API is rate-limited per address). Where it leads is `UpdateState`: with 自动下载更新 (`Settings::auto_download`) on and an app that can replace itself (`updater::target`: a `.app` bundle on macOS, the folder of `WispStudy.exe` on Windows, in a place that can be written), `download_update` fetches the release's package in the background (`wisp_net::download_release`, held against the release's `SHA256SUMS`) and `updater::stage` unpacks it into `.wisp-study-update` beside the app (macOS: the bundle out of the DMG, which must meet the running app's designated requirement; Windows: the zip's files, for a portable folder and for a copy the setup installed alike; after such an update `updater::tidy` writes the new version into the copy's entry among the system's apps). Nothing of the running app has changed then: `Ready` asks once (`offer_update`, a `Modal::Confirm` whose command is `Cmd::InstallUpdate`; a toast when another dialog is open), and 设置 › 关于 keeps 重启并更新. `install_update` is two renames (`updater::install`: the app in use steps aside into that folder, the new one takes its place), a start of the new app once this one is gone, and `cx.quit()`; the next start clears the folder (`updater::tidy`). Any failure falls back to `Available`, whose row offers the download again, or the release page where the app cannot replace itself (Linux, a build from `target/`). A package downloaded and not installed is fetched again after a restart.

The session (`Session` in core's `settings.rs`, `session.json` beside the settings): `Workspace::keep_session` observes the workspace and writes the open library, its page (`Page::key`), the paper on show, its tab and whether it is wide or in reading mode whenever one of them changed, so quitting, an update and a crash all leave it behind; `start_session` (from `main.rs`, once the window is there) goes back there through the same commands a click runs (`restore_session`), unless 回到上次的界面 (`Settings::restore_session`) is off or the start is scripted. The manuscript of 写作 needs no keeping: `Writing::latest` already opens the one last written.

## PDF import

PDF import is a run (`Library::import`, an `ImportRun`): a folder dropped or picked (添加文献 › 导入文件夹…) is expanded by `import::folder_pdfs`, each folder between it and a PDF becoming a tag of that paper (merged into a paper already held); a PDF the library already holds byte for byte under another name is the paper that holds it (`Store::paper_holding`, after `existing_paper` found nothing by DOI, PMID or title: without a DOI nothing else says the two are one); `import_paths` reads `IMPORT_WORKERS` files side by side (and `extract_missing` re-reads up to `READ_WORKERS` full texts at once), `add_pdf` adds a row for each, the sidebar shows the progress and then the summary until it is put away, `Modal::ImportReport` the rows. One file alone is a toast instead.

通过 DOI / PMID 添加 takes a pasted list too (`lookup::parse_identifiers`; a word that is neither stops the lot before anything is asked): `IMPORT_WORKERS` lookups side by side, `Workspace::added_by_id` adds each (`Store::import_papers`, so a paper already held is skipped and not an error) and counts it in the dialog's `IdRun`. With the last one the dialog closes with a toast, or stays with the identifiers nothing came of left in its field and the reasons under it. Closing the dialog stops the rest; what was added stays. One identifier alone behaves as before (the paper is selected; one already held is said in the dialog). The web addresses among the pasted words (`webpage::is_address`) always go to the background: the dialog closes, `import_urls` joins the import's run (`ImportRun::urls`, counted in `ids` as well) and takes them one after another through `library_tool("import_url")`, so on a connection of its own and only while the same library is open; `url_row` turns each answer into an `ImportRow`, whose `no_pdf` holds why a paper came without its PDF.

## Libraries to and from another Wisp Study

`Cmd::ExportLibrary(id, attachments)` (a library card's menu, the library's 导出 menu and the palette, each with PDFs and without) asks for a path with `prompt_for_new_path`, adds `.wispstudy` when missing and runs `archive::export` off the main thread; the open library's `info` is preferred to the home page's copy. `Cmd::ImportLibrary` (the home page's 导入文献库 and the palette) and a `.wispstudy` dropped anywhere (`import_paths` takes them out first, also with no library open) go to `Workspace::import_library`, which runs `archive::import` in the background and lists the libraries again; the library is not opened.

## Keyboard, right button and selection (`context.rs`, `select.rs`)

The keyboard and the right button (`src/context.rs`): the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End, Enter and Delete are fixed bindings in the `Workspace` context (`shortcuts::rebuild`) that end in `Workspace::nav`: in an open menu they move along its items, else they move in the papers on show (全部文献 and 筛选; left and right turn the pages of a PDF on show, else move a screenful), Enter opens the PDF, Delete or ⌘⌫ moves the paper to the trash and the one that takes its row is selected. A text field with the keyboard hears them first; a one-line or empty field passes Up and Down on. A right click is `.on_mouse_down(MouseButton::Right, self.on_context(MenuKind::…, cx))`, its items in `context_menu` (`Menu::breaks` are the lines between groups, `Menu::paper` the row marked meanwhile): papers, trashed papers, files, tags (rename and remove: `Workspace::retag`), past conversations, answers.

Several papers at once (`Library::marked`, 全部文献 only): a click with ⌘ / Ctrl adds or takes one (`Workspace::mark`), with Shift the stretch from `Library::anchor`; Shift with an arrow moves `Library::cursor`, ⌘A marks the list on show, Esc lets go. More than one marked puts the detail panel away, and showing one paper (`select`) ends the marking. A right click on a marked row is `MenuKind::Papers`; its commands end in `Workspace::run_batch` (`AskPapers`, `StarPapers`, `ReadPapers`, `TagPapers`, `FindPdfsOf`, `AskTrashPapers` / `TrashPapers`). `AskPapers` starts a new conversation with the library about those papers alone: `Library::asked`, read through `Library::scope_papers` wherever the scope of a library question is shown or built, and emptied by the next new conversation.

Text that is not an answer or a field is selected through `Workspace::selectable(key, text, cx)` (`src/select.rs`, `Workspace::picked`): drag, double click for a word, triple for all, ⌘C or a right click to copy. A double click picks the same word everywhere a text is read: `wisp_core::selection::word_at` (letters, digits and the marks that join them; on the mark or space right after a word, that word) is used here, in an answer (`markdown::Pointer::Word`: done at once and not held, so a slip of the hand before the release is no drag that cuts the word short, and the press stops there, since the window's own press handler ends a selection that is done) and on a PDF page (`pdftext::word_marks`, the same rule over a line's glyphs). The manuscript and the text fields keep ICU's words (`selection::word`). While the button is held a layer over the window follows the pointer, as for the edges. Use it for any text a reader may want to copy.

Selecting in an answer: `markdown::render` takes a `Selecting`, keeps the `TextLayout` of every piece it draws and reports where the pointer is as a `Place` (piece, byte; of pieces as near up and down, the cells of a table's row, the one nearest across); the selection is `Chat::picked`, its text `markdown::text_between`. While an answer holds the selection, its measuring canvas also hears the pointer's moves and release outside the answer (`window.on_mouse_event`): a drag back to the start easily overshoots the text, and a release not heard would leave the selection held, with no bar. What can be done with it stands in a bar by the selection, not under the answer, whose end may be screens away: 引用追问 (`Cmd::QuotePicked`: the text as a `>` quotation after what the box held, the box focused), 复制, and where they apply 所选插入稿件 and a review's buttons; the right-click menu (`MenuKind::Answer`) names the first two the same. Placing it takes a frame: a `canvas` after the text reports where the selection's ends were drawn (`markdown::Drawn`), and `chat.rs` keeps that as a `chat::Spot` in `Chat::spot`, in terms scrolling leaves alone (from the answer's corner, and the answer's corner in the scrolled content); a canvas in the bar keeps its size in `Chat::bar_size`. Each frame `Spot::bar_at` adds the scroll offset of the moment and `chat::beside_selection` picks the place in the visible conversation: under the line it ends on, else over its first line, else at the view's foot, inside the view and none while the selection is out of it. The bar is `deferred` so that the answers after it do not paint over it, and blocks the pointer except the wheel. The selection ends with Esc, a new one, or a press anywhere else (`Library::unpick`, from the workspace's own `on_mouse_down`; one still held is being made and stays).

## The command palette (`palette.rs`)

`Workspace::palette` is the palette while it is open (⌘K / Ctrl+K, the shortcut `palette`; the same key or Esc puts it away): a layer over the window like a dialog's, drawn by `render_palette` between the dialogs and the menus. Its text field is `choosing`, so the arrow keys, Enter and Esc come to `palette_event` as `InputEvent`s and never reach the list under it; while it is open the other shortcuts wait (`Workspace::shortcut`), and it does not open over a dialog. `palette_rows` makes the rows anew with every change of the text, never in a frame: the commands on offer where the window is (the views and pages of the open library, what 添加文献 offers, export, the other libraries, the appearance, the pages of 设置), then, once something is typed, up to 50 papers of the open library that `filter::says` holds the words (what the list's search box looks through, whatever the filters). A fixed label is found in both languages (`t(label)` and the key); a label put together passes its words in the other language as `also`. Every row ends in a `Cmd` carried out by `palette_take` (a paper is `Cmd::OpenImported`: it opens beside the page in view), so a new command is one more tuple there and nothing else; from 设置, anything but a page or a choice of the settings closes them first.

## Layout: panels, sidebar, list head, reading mode

The detail panel's width is `Settings::detail_width` (0 is `DETAIL_W`), dragged by its left edge (`detail.rs`). An element only hears the pointer moving over itself, so while an edge is held (`Workspace::resizing`, an `Edge`) a layer over the whole window takes the moves (`home.rs`); do the same for any other drag. A tap is over before that layer is drawn, so each edge also hears its own mouse-up.

The library's sidebar is `Settings::sidebar_width` (0 is `SIDEBAR_W`) and `Settings::sidebar_hidden`: its right edge is dragged (`Workspace::drag_sidebar`; pulled in past half of `SIDEBAR_MIN` it is put away, back out and it returns), clicked twice it is put away, and `Cmd::ToggleSidebar` (the button at the top left, the shortcut `sidebar`) puts it away or brings it back. Widths are computed from `Workspace::sidebar_width`, never from the constant. Its rows are three groups (`render_sidebar`): the views of the one list of papers (`VIEWS`: 全部文献, 最近添加, 未读, 收藏, 缺 PDF), then the pages where the work is done, 助手 and a review's path in its order (检索与导入, 筛选, 提取, 写作), then the tags, and under the line what is managed (自定义词条, 回收站, 设置). A number is shown only when it is above 0. What waits for a decision is 筛选's number and the 收录 filter, not a view. The two top-left buttons (`render_corner`) are drawn in the sidebar or, while it is away, over the list at the same place, level with macOS's traffic lights. Its arrow is the only way back in the sidebar and goes up one level: 文献库 from a manuscript's outline (`Cmd::LeaveWrite`: the page changes with the sidebar, to the one 写作 was opened from), 文献库 from the narrow list beside a paper asked about (`Cmd::LeaveAsk`), 所有文献库 from the library. 设置 has its 返回 in the same corner. The arrow does nothing on the second click of a double click: the arrow one level up has by then taken its place under the pointer.

The head of the paper list (`render_papers`) keeps its height (`papers_head`) however narrow the list is. Under `roomy` the three buttons are `ui::tool_button`s (the icon, the words in a tooltip, `ui::tip`) and the search box says only 搜索…. The filter chips are measured (`shape_line`); where they do not all fit the row is folded: the set ones first, as many as fit, `+N` for the rest. Under the pointer a card with all of them lies over the row and the list (`Workspace::filters_over`: the row's hover and the card's; `Menu::filter` keeps it under a filter's choices), drawn as the column's last child, its first chips where the row's are, shown after a moment by its animation. `filters_drawn` forgets the pointer in a frame that does not draw the folded row, since `on_hover` only says a change.

The table's columns (`columns`): the star and the title, then 期刊, 影响因子, 分区 (the partition `Settings::journal_zone` names, set in 设置 › 外观; `zone_sort`, `zone_text`; a journal on a warning list is drawn in `Theme::warning()`), 年份, the custom fields that are `in_table`, 全文, 阅读, 收录, as many as leave the title `TITLE_MIN`. Each has a key (`ColumnKind::key`: `journal`, `field:<id>`, …). `Settings::column_widths` holds the widths the reader gave by key, `Settings::columns_hidden` the built-in columns put away (`COLUMNS`); a field's column is its own `in_table`. The edge at a column's left in the row of names is held and moved (`Edge::Column`, `Workspace::column_drag`, `drag_column`; the pure part is `dragged`): the column trades width with the one before it, or with the title, which has what the others leave; clicked twice the column has its own width again. A right click on the names is `MenuKind::Columns` (`Cmd::ToggleColumn`, `Cmd::ToggleFieldInTable`, `Cmd::ResetColumnWidths`). Columns the reader left on show that the width has no room for are not dropped without a word: `fitted` hands back their names, and the title's cell in the row of names ends in `+N 列` (the names on hover, the columns' menu on a press). It stands in the title's cell because that is the one that gives, so no column moves. `render_rows` measures what each cell shows (`shape_line` with the row's text style); a cell that cuts it has a tooltip with the whole: the title cell a `PaperTip` (title, the line under it, all tags), a status chip in a narrowed column its label. Truncated text ends in an ellipsis through the patch to gpui's text measure (`vendor/PATCHES.md`, 23).

Reading mode (`Library::reading`, `Cmd::ToggleReading`: the button on the PDF's toolbar, the shortcut `reading`, Esc to leave before Esc closes the paper): `detail_width` is then the window's and `render_library` draws the detail panel alone, with a one-line header and no left edge. Nothing is made anew, so `Library::pdf` keeps its place and `paper_chat` its conversation and unsent text; leaving the paper, changing the page or the search shortcut ends it. The conversation's width beside the PDF is `Settings::chat_width` (0 is a share of the panel; `Workspace::chat_width`, `split_chat_width` in `library.rs`), dragged by the edge between the two (`Edge::Split`, `drag_split`). The two change places with the button in the paper's header row (`split-swap`, `Cmd::SwapPanes`, also in the command palette and the debug step `swap`): `Settings::pdf_first` puts the PDF at the left, remembered for every paper and after a restart. `render_detail` only draws the same two panes in the other order (the chat pane carries the line between them on its side), so nothing is made anew: `Library::pdf` keeps its page and zoom, `paper_chat` its messages, stream and the text being typed. `chat_width` stays the conversation's width on either side, so swapping keeps the widths; the edge sits at `width - chat_width` and `drag_split` measures the conversation from the panel's right edge (`split_chat_reach`), under the same limits. The button is in the header row the two panes share, not in either pane, so it stays put when they swap; there is none while the conversation floats over the PDF (`Settings::floating`).

## Asking about a paper and the floating conversation (`library.rs`, `floating.rs`)

Asking about a paper lays the window out for it (`Workspace::asking`, in `library.rs`): the sidebar is away, the papers on show are a narrow list at the left (`render_ask_list`: the corner's two buttons, the search box, a two-line title and a line of details per paper; `Settings::ask_list_width`, 0 is `ASK_LIST_W`, dragged by the detail panel's left edge), and the conversation with the paper's PDF to its right has the rest (`detail_width`). Nothing is stored for it: it follows from 提问 being the tab and `Library::split` (on by default; `Cmd::BesidePdf`, the x on the PDF's bar puts the PDF away and so ends it for the papers after as well, 对照 PDF brings it back). Beside the list the paper's one-line header is the title strip itself (`in_strip` in `render_detail`; on Windows it stops short of the window's buttons), so 退出提问 is level with the list's arrow. `Cmd::LeaveAsk` (Esc before Esc closes the paper, 退出提问 in the one-line header, the list's ← 文献库, the sidebar's button or shortcut) goes back to the tab it was come to from (`Library::ask_from`). On a page whose list is not the papers' (筛选, 助手, 写作), or in a window narrower than `ASK_LIST_MIN` + `detail::SPLIT_MIN`, there is no list and the paper has the window. A paper without a PDF keeps the layout, with 添加 PDF / 查找开放全文 at the right, so the arrow keys do not change the layout from paper to paper. The table and the narrow list share `Library::table_scroll`; `render_library` scrolls to the selected paper when the layout changes (`Library::ask_drawn`), and row heights come from `Workspace::row_height`. A citation followed from that conversation, a passage or a picture sent from the PDF, all end there.

The paper's conversation can float over its PDF instead (`src/floating.rs`, `Settings::floating`, the geometry in `wisp_core::floating`): where the conversation would stand beside the PDF, the PDF takes the whole panel and the same `Chat` and input are drawn in a window over it (the PDF's bar of tools stays clear). The title bar moves it, the bottom-right corner sizes it (`MIN_WIDTH` / `MIN_HEIGHT`), 分栏 goes back beside, the minus folds it into a round button that a drag moves and a click opens (`is_drag`: farther than `DRAG_SLOP`). Place, size and folding are saved; `fit` keeps what is drawn inside the area, which is measured as it is drawn (`Library::float_area`), and leaves the stored place alone, so a window grown back finds it. A held title bar, corner or button is `Library::float_drag`, heard by a layer over the whole window (`render_float_layer`, drawn in home.rs) like the edges. The window and button `occlude()`, so the PDF under them hears neither wheel nor press while the rest of it scrolls and selects as before. A passage or picture sent from the PDF opens a folded conversation (`unfold_chat` in `show_paper_chat`). The switch to floating is 悬浮 in the conversation's header (`render_float_switch`).

## PDF view (`pdf.rs`)

Where a file is read at (#194): `Library::file_places` holds each file's `PdfView::place` (the page at the top and how far down it; a view not open yet gives the place it is to open at), and a viewer made for a file `resume`s there. `Library::keep_places` notes the viewers on show, there and in the library's `reading_places` table (`Store::save_reading_places`), whenever a paper is left (`select`), the library is closed (`close_library`) or the app quits (`on_app_quit` in `start_session`); `file_places` is read back from the table when the library opens. Zoom is not part of it: the fit is a habit that goes with the reader, a percentage belongs to one paper. 后退 / 前进 (`Cmd::StepHistory`, the shortcuts `back` / `forward`, ⌘[ / ⌘], and the mouse's side buttons, `MouseButton::Navigate` on the root in `home.rs`) walk `Library::back` / `forward`: `select` pushes the paper left (at most `HISTORY`) and clears `forward`, except while `Workspace::stepping` says the selection is itself a step; a paper taken away since is passed over.

`PdfView` draws pages at `Zoom` (fit width, fit page, or a percentage of the page's own size). `resize` is the one place that changes what decides the page size and keeps the point under an anchor where it was; pages are rendered at `render_scale` (a few steps, within `MAX_PAGE_PIXELS`) and kept within `KEEP_BYTES`. The wheel is heard in the capture phase by a canvas under the list (`PdfView::wheel`), since the list would scroll before a parent could zoom. A pinch on the trackpad is that same wheel with Ctrl: Windows hands it on so, and on macOS the vendored gpui does (`vendor/PATCHES.md`, 24), so the zoom follows the fingers about the pointer. Pages wider than their view have a bar at the view's foot (`sideways_bar`, one for each half of a split; `bar_thumb` is its geometry): it is there whenever `max_x` is more than nothing, so that there is more to either side can be seen, and its thumb is dragged (`bar_press`, `slide`; `PdfView::sliding`) or its track pressed. The press stops there, so no selection starts under the bar, and while the thumb is held the canvas's own listener hears the pointer wherever it goes. A fit follows the reading area: `render_pdf_tab` hands the view its width every frame (`set_width`, the height comes from layout), and `resize` keeps the top of the view on the same page and spot, so the window, the sidebar, the edges, reading mode and the split all need nothing of their own. The viewer split in two (`pdf::Split`: `Side`, `Stacked`; the toolbar's 分屏 menu, `MenuKind::PdfSplit` → `Cmd::PdfSplit` → `PdfView::set_split`, the debug step `pdfsplit:side|stacked|off`): the same `PdfView` draws its pages twice, a figure in one half and the page that speaks of it in the other. The split is the viewer's, for this paper: nothing is saved, and the next paper opens whole.

A half can show another file of the paper (#185): the main text in one, a supplement in the other, so a supplementary figure stays in view while the text is read. The 分屏 menu lists the paper's PDFs under its three ways (`Library::pdf_choices`, labelled as the toolbar's title names them: 另一半：附件 S1 · …; not while 译文 has the other half) and a choice is `Cmd::PdfBeside` → `Workspace::show_beside`. Each viewer has its own place, marks, selection, zoom and finder, so nothing of `PdfView` knows of the other, and its own split stays `Off` while it has a neighbour. `Library::pdf` stays the viewer worked in, so the toolbar, the keys, a selection sent to a conversation, a cut and a citation followed need nothing of their own: a press or a wheel in a viewer emits `PdfEvent::Worked`, and `Workspace::work_in` swaps `pdf` and `beside.view` (with their subscriptions) when it came from the other, flips `first` so neither moves on screen, and points `read_file` at its file. `show_file` (the title's menu, `Cmd::ReadFile`, `Cmd::GotoFile` for a citation, `Cmd::FigureSource`) goes to the half showing the file asked for, and otherwise changes the file of the half worked in only. Every subscription hands `pdf_event` its viewer, so a quotation names the file it came from and `change_pdf_annotation` gives the marks to the viewer showing their file. Choosing the file worked in is the viewer split on itself again; 不分屏 closes the other half (`close_beside`, its place kept in `file_places`), as does turning 译文 on. `Library::close_pdf` puts both away wherever the viewer was dropped (another paper, a file removed or added). Debug step: `pdfbeside:<n>`.

对照译文 (#178, `src/facing.rs`; `Cmd::ToggleFacing`, `FacingRun`, `FacingStop`): 译文 on the PDF's bar shows the pages twice, the original and a copy with each paragraph replaced by its translation, like pdf2zh. `Library::facing` is the switch and stays on from paper to paper, and from the PDF tab to the PDF beside or under the paper's conversation (`Library::facing_shown`: the switch while `pdf_on_show`; turned on there, it does not move to the PDF tab; #193); `load_facing` (called by `ensure_pdf`, so it follows the file on show) reads the file's paragraphs in the background (`Library::facing_text`, a `Facing`: `translation::passages` and their keys), `rekey_facing` brings in what is kept, and `sync_facing` hands the viewer each page's `FacingPiece`s after every change. Selecting in a PDF: the worker hands out a page's glyphs (`pdftext::page_marks`, after the page is drawn), `PdfView` keeps a `Selection` of them on one page (`mark_at`, `mark_boxes`, `marks_text` in core; a double click takes `word_marks`, finished at once), and tells its owner through `PdfEvent` (copied; a passage for a conversation, kept in `Chat::quotes` and written into the question as `[P3 p.5 "…"]` by `ai::quoted_passage`; a piece of a page cut out as a picture; a passage to translate). 翻译 puts a card by the selection (`card_place` keeps it in view): `Workspace::translate_passage` goes through `translate_text` (`src/translate.rs`), the same path and cache as the title and abstract and the batch, and `sync_pdf_translation` hands the state back (`PdfView::show_translation`). Marks on a PDF (`wisp_core::annotation`): the selection's bar makes an underline or a highlight (`PdfEvent::Annotate`; a new highlight is the first of `annotation::COLORS`), drawn over the page in `Theme::underline_mark` / `Theme::highlight`, and every change goes through `Workspace::change_pdf_annotation`, which writes, reads the file's marks again and hands them to `PdfView::annotations_saved` (the scroll and zoom stay).
